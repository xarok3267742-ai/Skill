# Competitor research and product handoff

Research 5-8 direct or adjacent Google Play competitors for the selected countries, locales, form factors, and audience. Prefer current store listings, official product sites, observable app behavior, and recent review themes. Record the access date and source URL for every factual claim.

## Evidence matrix

Capture, where available:

- app, developer, listing URL, country/locale, category, update date, rating and review count;
- audience, primary promise, core journey, feature set, pricing/subscription/ads, permissions, and trust signals;
- icon, screenshot sequence, copy patterns, localization quality, TV/Wear presentation, and accessibility cues;
- repeated positive and negative review themes, with review date and market;
- evidence-backed gap, risk, or differentiation opportunity.

Use a real spreadsheet artifact when spreadsheet tooling is available. Label unavailable, estimated, or qualitative values. Do not infer keyword volume, revenue, installs, retention, or ranking difficulty without a cited data source.

## Synthesis

Separate observed facts, review-language themes, and product hypotheses. Select a defensible positioning and the smallest feature set that proves it. Do not reproduce a competitor's protected copy, branded assets, screenshots, or distinctive visual treatment.

Pass the approved synthesis to UX and implementation as:

- audience, job, entry point, and success event;
- differentiator and supporting product proof;
- shortest core journey and critical failure states;
- form-factor and localization constraints;
- measurable acceptance criteria and out-of-scope items.

Use `$visualize` only when a journey, information architecture, or system relationship benefits from a diagram. Use `$imagegen` for original concept art, icons, backgrounds, or feature graphics when helpful. Google Play screenshots must be captured from the tested application; never fabricate product UI with image generation.
