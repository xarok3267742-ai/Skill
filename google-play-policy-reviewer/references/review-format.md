# Policy review format

For each finding include:

- Severity: release blocker, high, medium, or low.
- Status: confirmed, likely, or cannot verify.
- Surface and evidence.
- Applicable official policy with link and access date.
- User/review impact.
- Remediation in code, declaration, listing, or policy document.
- Verification step after remediation.

Finish with a reconciliation table covering observed data behavior, permissions, SDKs, consent, privacy policy, Data safety, account deletion, monetization, audience, form factors, languages, reviewer access, and store claims. List unavailable evidence explicitly. Do not state “compliant” when material behavior could not be observed.
