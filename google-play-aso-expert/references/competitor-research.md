# Google Play competitor research

Select 5-8 direct or adjacent competitors by target country, language, audience, use case, and form factor. Avoid a sample made only of famous global apps when local competitors better represent user intent.

Use current Play listings as the primary store evidence. Supplement with official product pages and observable product behavior. Reviews are user reports, not verified facts; group recurring themes and retain dates instead of treating one review as representative.

Record in a spreadsheet when available:

- app, developer, URL, market/locale, category, update date, rating, and review count;
- title, short-description promise, keyword/intent themes, feature proof, pricing, subscriptions, ads, and trust cues;
- icon recognition, screenshot narrative, feature graphic, localization, TV/Wear assets, and accessibility cues;
- recurring praise, complaints, missing capabilities, and switching objections;
- evidence-backed gap and differentiation opportunity for the user's product.

Attach a source URL and access date to factual values. Mark unavailable data and qualitative judgments. Never claim hidden keyword volume, revenue, conversion, retention, install counts, or ranking difficulty without an attributable source.

Do not copy listing copy, screenshots, icons, names, characters, layouts, or distinctive branded elements. Competitor research should clarify user expectations and gaps, not produce an imitation.
