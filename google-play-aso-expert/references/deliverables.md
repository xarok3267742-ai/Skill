# ASO deliverables

Provide only sections relevant to the request:

1. Inputs and assumptions.
2. Audience and positioning in one concise statement.
3. Competitor matrix and keyword clusters with market, intent, product relevance, evidence source, access date, and priority.
4. Metadata variants with exact character counts.
5. Screenshot sequence: purpose, headline, visual proof, and target objection for each frame.
6. Icon or feature-graphic hypotheses tied to recognition and category conventions.
7. Localization map: default/fallback locale, interface/content/listing coverage, adapted terms, RTL or typography needs, avoided literal translations, and compliance sensitivities.
8. Experiment backlog ranked by expected impact, confidence, and effort.
9. Measurement plan: primary metric, guardrails, segment, duration logic, and decision rule.

Use installs or conversion as outcomes; ranking movement alone is diagnostic. Flag low-volume tests that cannot reach a reliable conclusion and propose a practical alternative.

Only use screenshots captured from the tested product. Keep provenance for generated icon or feature-graphic concepts and never depict unavailable functionality.
