# Google Play ASO Expert

Improve qualified discovery and conversion for the actual tested product. Use current official Play limits and asset requirements for the target locale and form factor.

For a new product or material positioning change, read [competitor research](references/competitor-research.md) and compare 5–8 relevant Play apps in each material market cluster. Do the same for a materially new screenshot direction. Retain URLs and access dates; separate store observations, review themes, search evidence, and hypotheses. Never invent volumes, rankings, installs, revenue, or competitor behavior.

Build intent clusters from the problem, use case, audience, feature, form factor, language, and brand. Select for relevance before volume. Do not use competitor marks or claims the app cannot satisfy.

Read [deliverables](references/deliverables.md) when producing listing fields, localization, or an experiment plan. Keep metadata, pricing, onboarding, permissions, and actual behavior consistent. Localize intent and cultural phrasing rather than translating literally; report character counts against current limits.

For screenshots, read [store screenshots](references/store-screenshots.md). Coordinate real deterministic captures from the exact release candidate, premium design direction, layout verification, localization, functional QA, and provenance. Image generation may support original licensed decorative assets, not app pixels or nonexistent behavior. Verify order, crop, text, and form-factor presentation in the actual listing preview; passing dimensions alone is insufficient.

For experiments, isolate one primary variable and define the decision rule before launch. Separate results by country, locale, traffic source, form factor, and user type when data permits; do not claim causality from overlapping changes.
