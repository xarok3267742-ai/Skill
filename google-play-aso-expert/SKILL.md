# Google Play ASO Expert

Improve qualified discovery and install conversion without misrepresenting the product. Treat market, locale, audience, form factor, brand, actual build behavior, traffic source, and competitor evidence as inputs. Verify current Google Play field limits, asset rules, and policy-sensitive requirements from official sources at execution time.

## Research before writing

For new products or positioning changes, read [references/competitor-research.md](references/competitor-research.md) and compare 5-8 relevant Google Play apps in each material market cluster. Separate product facts, store observations, review-language themes, search evidence, and hypotheses. Do not invent keyword volume, ranking difficulty, installs, revenue, or competitor behavior.

Build intent clusters around the problem, use case, feature, audience, form factor, language, and brand. Select terms for relevance first. Do not use keywords, competitor trademarks, or claims the app cannot satisfy.

## Produce the listing

- Keep every field within current locale-specific limits and show character counts.
- Put the clearest differentiator and product proof early; use natural, specific language.
- Align metadata, screenshots, icon, feature graphic, onboarding, permissions, pricing, and actual functionality.
- Localize user intent and cultural phrasing rather than translating word for word; define default and fallback locales explicitly.
- Use actual tested-app captures for screenshots. Image generation may create original icon or feature-graphic concepts, never fake functionality.
- Apply current phone/tablet, TV, and Wear OS listing requirements separately.
- Avoid keyword blocks, unverifiable superlatives, misleading ratings, competitor marks, and prohibited calls to action.

Read [references/deliverables.md](references/deliverables.md) for the output and experiment structure.

## Evaluate

Separate acquisition metrics by country, language, traffic source, form factor, and new/returning user where data allows. Do not attribute causality to simultaneous product, price, metadata, or creative changes. Recommend one primary variable per experiment and define a decision rule before launch.
